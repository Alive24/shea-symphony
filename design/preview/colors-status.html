Status Signal System

Use status color to clarify lane health and operator risk. Each status must stay legible in Daylight review mode and Night cockpit mode.

Daylight

Starbucks Green for pass/ready, Yellow for warning, Red for destructive or unsafe states.

SuccessReady, review pass, healthy backend, canonical checkout ready. #00754A
WarningNeeds attention, mergeability unknown, review retry, dry-run caution. #fbbc05
DangerUnsafe continuation, failed checks, missing evidence, destructive action blocked. #c82014
AccentCurrent selection, issue tag, focused decision, brand signal. #006241
